Ukrainian developer of strike, loitering-munition and cargo UAVs. I joined as the only software engineer, built the software and autonomy team from scratch and hired its first two engineers. NADIR, SEXTANT and ORRERY are my own IP, used by the company; code walkthrough on request.

\project{NADIR --- Visual-Inertial Odometry}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Made the company's drones fly without GPS on \$15 of added hardware: visual-inertial odometry from one downward camera gives each drone the position and velocity it would otherwise take from satellites, both for holding position and for guided flight.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}
\project{SEXTANT --- Long-Range Terrain Navigation}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Extended GPS-denied flight to long range: the drone finds its absolute position and heading by matching the shape of the surrounding terrain against a terrain model, which keeps working in the snow, foliage and changing light that defeat matching by appearance.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}
\project{ORRERY --- Standalone Flight Simulator}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Cut the cost and risk of flight testing with a simulator in which NADIR and SEXTANT must pass their acceptance tests before they reach a real drone; any ArduPilot version, camera or vehicle is set up by configuration, without code changes.
\end{itemize}
