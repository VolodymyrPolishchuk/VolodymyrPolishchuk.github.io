\project{NADIR}{position hold for ArduPilot from one downward camera}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Goal: reliable position hold without GPS, from one downward camera on a \$15 companion computer.
    \item Techniques: visual-inertial odometry. Ground features, tracked and corrected for the drone's own rotation, give metric velocity; an anchor on ground features holds position without drift; ArduPilot's EKF fuses both in place of GPS.
\end{itemize}

\project{SEXTANT}{long-range navigation by terrain geometry}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Goal: absolute position and heading without GPS over long range, where odometry alone drifts.
    \item Techniques: terrain-referenced navigation by geometry rather than appearance, so snow, foliage and sun do not break it. The forward camera's skyline, matched against a terrain model, gives position and heading; downward motion stereo measures the relief below; ArduPilot's EKF fuses both.
\end{itemize}

\project{ORRERY}{standalone simulator for camera-based flight}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Goal: fly camera-based navigation on the desk, with the aircraft's sensor and timing effects, before it flies for real.
    \item Techniques: a Godot simulator coupled step for step to ArduPilot SITL, with cameras modelled as real sensors. Any SITL build, any camera and the vehicle are set by configuration.
\end{itemize}
