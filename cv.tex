\section*{EXPERIENCE}

\role{Applied Technology Collective}{Head of Software \& Autonomy}{Kyiv, Ukraine | 2026 -- Present}
Ukrainian developer of strike, loitering-munition and cargo UAVs. Joined as the only software engineer, built the software and autonomy function from zero and hired its first two engineers. NADIR, SEXTANT and ORRERY are my own IP, used by the company; code walkthrough on request.

\project{NADIR --- GPS-Denied Position Hold (In Production)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Freed the company's drones from depending on GPS to hold position with a visual-inertial navigation system: one downward camera and the autopilot's IMU give the velocity and position ArduPilot flies on in place of satellites.
    \item Kept the hardware to \$15 per drone by running the whole system on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, shipped as a sealed product that operators calibrate in the field and that refuses to arm until the setup is safe.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}
\project{SEXTANT --- Long-Range Terrain Navigation (Rolling Out)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Extended GPS-denied flight from holding position to navigating over long range by fixing absolute position and heading from the terrain itself: the forward camera's skyline is matched against a terrain model, and downward motion stereo reads the relief below.
    \item Chose terrain geometry over image matching so that navigation survives snow, foliage and changing light, the conditions that defeat appearance-based systems.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}
\project{ORRERY --- Standalone Flight Simulator}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Cut the cost and risk of flight testing with the simulator in which NADIR and SEXTANT pass their acceptance tests before reaching an aircraft, coupled to ArduPilot SITL with cameras modelled as real sensors.
    \item Made it independent of our own stack: any ArduPilot SITL build, any camera and any vehicle plug in through configuration alone.
\end{itemize}
