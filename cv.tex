\project{NADIR --- GPS-Denied Position Hold (In Production)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Freed the company's drones from depending on GPS to hold position with visual-inertial navigation: one downward camera and the autopilot's IMU give the velocity and position ArduPilot flies on in place of satellites.
    \item Kept the hardware to \$15 per drone, shipped as a sealed product that operators calibrate in the field and that refuses to arm until the setup is safe.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}
\project{SEXTANT --- Long-Range Terrain Navigation (Rolling Out)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Extended GPS-denied flight to long range by fixing absolute position and heading from the terrain's geometry, matched against a terrain model, so that navigation survives the snow, foliage and changing light that defeat image matching.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}
\project{ORRERY --- Standalone Flight Simulator}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Cut the cost and risk of flight testing with a simulator in which NADIR and SEXTANT pass their acceptance tests before reaching an aircraft; any ArduPilot SITL build, camera and vehicle plug in through configuration alone.
\end{itemize}
